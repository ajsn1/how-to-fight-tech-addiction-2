Tips to Prevent Technology Addiction

  1. Limit daily screen time.
  2. Turn off unnecessary notifications.
  3. Take regular breaks from screens.
  4. Avoid devices before bedtime.
  5. Spend time on offline hobbies.
  6. Create technology-free areas.
  7. Use social media in moderation.
  8. Meet friends and family in person.
  9. Track your screen time.
  10. Use technology purposefully.
